Front-Running TWAPs on Hyperliquid

A forced, public and signed order flow. Price pressure scales with the TWAP's weight in global volume and lasts exactly as long as it runs. A frozen strategy passed a pre-registered out-of-sample test (t = 4.59), then the edge was arbitraged away within a year: same-side followers went from 14 % to 66 % of signals.
